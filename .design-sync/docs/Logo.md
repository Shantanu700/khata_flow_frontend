# Logo

The KhaataFlow mark: a white disc with a book glyph and a lime status dot, beside the "KhaataFlow" wordmark.

## When to use
Top-left of every screen, at the start of the header row.

## What you provide
Nothing; it takes no props. The wordmark hides visually under 640px but stays readable by screen readers.

## Rules
- There is no separate logo file: the mark is this component (a lucide book icon in a white disc). Don't redraw it.
- Keep it on `canvas`; the dot's ring is the canvas colour.
